About Health Connect

Health Connect is a one-stop healthcare portal that helps individuals, families, and companies navigate insurance assistance, home health care, and diagnostic services in the Philippines.

Our Mission

We aim to make healthcare more accessible and easier to understand by connecting you with trusted insurance partners, home health providers, and diagnostic centers. Instead of navigating multiple websites and contact points, Health Connect gives you a single place to inquire, compare, and schedule.

What We Do
  • Assist with health insurance quotations from multiple providers.
  • Help coordinate home health care services such as private nurses and caregivers.
  • Provide an easy way to request and schedule lab tests and radiology procedures.
  • Connect you with the right partner based on your budget, coverage needs, and location.
Who We Serve
  • Individuals looking for personal or family health coverage.
  • SMEs and companies needing employee benefit programs.
  • Senior citizens, OFWs, and other special segments.
  • Patients who prefer home-based care or assisted recovery at home.
Why Use Health Connect?
  • Single portal for multiple insurance brands.
  • Assistance from real coordinators behind the platform.
  • Simple online forms for quotations and scheduling.
  • Aligned with Philippine healthcare providers.
