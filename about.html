About Health Connect

Health Connect Services and Consultancy OPC started in March 2024 and it is owned by Mr. Adrian Robert Poblete a registered nurse and a licensed non-life insurance agent.

Health Connect was created to provide unapparelled services to individual, family and corporate group for their insurance and health care needs.

Aside from that we link and connect our client to a comprehensive personalized services that truly gives them piece of mind.

As a Servicing provider we collaborate and partner with numerous clinics, experienced Doctors and other Health care associations including all health care professionals.

Heath Connect is committed to provide quality and personalized services designed to respond to our clients’ needs.

Why Use Health Connect?
  • Single portal for multiple insurance brands.
  • Assistance from real coordinators behind the platform.
  • Simple online forms for quotations and scheduling.
  • Aligned with Philippine healthcare providers.
