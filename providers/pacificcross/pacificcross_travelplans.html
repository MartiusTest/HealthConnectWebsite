ShortSecure Benefits
Coverage for land or sea trips.
  • Pacific Cross ShortSecure plan is specifically designed for travels which are only by land and/or by sea.
  • Up to PHP500,000 Medical Treatment due to accident and specified illnesses.
  • With Personal Accident Coverage for accidental bodily injury.

E-mail traveldirect_team@pacificcross.com.ph to inquire or purchase ShortSecure.

Benefit Highlights
  • Open to persons age six (6) weeks old to 75 years old.
  • Coverage shall commence at the time of departure of the Insured Person from the point of origin which may be the accommodation site such as place of residence or employment, hotels, universities or schools, or the terminal wherein the transport vehicle to be used for the land or water trip will set off and ends on the expiry date or upon arrival at the point of origin, whichever occurs first.
  • Coverage is allowed for travel using rented, public or company vehicles as well as private cars even when the Insured Person is driving.
  • ShortSecure Group Plans are for a minimum number of 30 persons per trip who shall be leaving on the same day and with the same itinerary.

  • Our Travelsafe coverage that offers Dollar plans for non-residents and expatriates visiting the Philippines.
  • Up to USD50,000 Medical Treatment, with benefits for In-Patient and Out-Patient care and more.
  • Extensive coverage for Personal Accident, Flight and Baggage Delays, Trip Cancellation, and Emergency Assistance among other.

E-mail traveldirect_team@pacificcross.com.ph to inquire or purchase Worldwide Elite-Inbound.

Benefit Highlights

Pacific Cross offers an inbound travel insurance plan designed for non-residents and expatriates visiting the Philippines, providing up to $50,000 in medical coverage. The plan includes benefits for In-Patient and Out-Patient care, Emergency Dental treatment, and COVID-19-related medical expenses. It also protects against common travel disruptions, such as flight delays and baggage loss. With financial security for both health and travel issues, visitors can explore the Philippines with peace of mind.
