Long Term Health Care

Kaiser Long-Term Care is a unique program that blends healthcare protection, savings, and investment designed to support your retirement and long-term medical needs.

Key Benefits:
  • Guaranteed Health Coverage
  • Yearly Health Benefits
  • Lifetime Health Benefits upon Maturity
  • Hospitalization & Medical Benefits
  • Cash Values & Investment Growth
  • Bonus Benefits (if no hospitalization is used)
Who is it For?
  • Individuals planning for future healthcare costs
  • Families preparing long-term protection
  • Professionals preparing retirement health funds
  • Filipinos working abroad (OFWs) who want long-term stability
