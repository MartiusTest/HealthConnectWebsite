DigiMed Consult (Single-use)
Single-use digital consultation via HeyPhil app
₱450.00
What’s Covered

🩺 Single-Use DigiMed Consult
Need quick, one-time medical advice from a doctor? This plan is for you.

  • Perfect for ages 1 year old and above.
  • Includes one (1) digital consultation with a licensed PhilCare doctor through the HeyPhil app — no clinic visit required.
Benefit Coverage
  • ✔ Covers consultation with:
    • General Practitioner / Family Medicine
What’s Included
  • Online medical consultation via HeyPhil App
  • Medical advice and assessment
  • Electronic prescription, if needed
  • Referral for lab tests or specialist consults, if required
Limitations / Exclusions
  • Designed for non-emergency medical concerns only.
  • Not intended for life-threatening or urgent cases (e.g., severe chest pain, stroke symptoms, major trauma).

For emergency or life-threatening situations, members should proceed immediately to the nearest emergency room.

DigiMed Plus Consult (Single-use)
Single-use specialist video consultation
₱550.00
What’s Covered
  • One-on-one secure video consultation with a specialist doctor.
  • Includes:
    • E-prescription
    • Lab or diagnostic requests
    • Specialist referrals
    • Interpretation of existing lab results
  • Accepts non-emergency pre-existing conditions.
Available Specializations
  • Pediatrics
  • Surgery (ENT, Orthopedics, Eye, etc.)
  • OB-Gyne
  • Urology
  • Rehabilitation Medicine

This plan is ideal when you specifically need a specialist’s opinion that can be safely delivered via teleconsultation.

Not Covered
  • Emergency cases
  • Maternity concerns
  • Dental consults
  • Psychiatric or mental health consults
  • Behavioral or developmental disorders
  • Post-surgery complications
  • COVID clearance or “fit-to-work” exams

For urgent or life-threatening symptoms, members should proceed directly to an emergency room instead of using teleconsult.

Unli-DigiMed Consult
Unlimited online consultations
₱999.00
What’s Covered
  • Unlimited online consultations with licensed doctors.
  • Medical assessment and digital prescription.
  • Lab test endorsements, if needed.
  • Specialist referrals, if required.
  • Lab result interpretation.
  • Covers non-emergency pre-existing conditions.
Covered Specializations
  • General Physician / Family Medicine

Unli-DigiMed is ideal for ongoing primary care, follow-ups, and non-urgent medical concerns that can be managed through teleconsult.

Not Covered
  • Emergency-related cases (e.g., heart attack, stroke, trauma injuries, severe pain, breathing issues, major burns, animal bites)
  • Maternity-related cases
  • Dental consultations
  • Mental health and psychiatric conditions
  • Developmental or behavioral disorders
  • Skin conditions (dermatology)
  • Severe or complex illnesses needing a specialist
  • “Fit-to-work” or medical clearance certificates

For symptoms that may be life-threatening or rapidly worsening, members should go directly to the nearest emergency room instead of using Unli-DigiMed.
