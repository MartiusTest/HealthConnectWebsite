IndieGO 40
Up to ₱40,000 single-use emergency & hospitalization coverage
₱1,750.00
What’s Covered?
  • 🧒 Emergency and Hospitalization Coverage up to ₱40,000
  • ✔ Single-use coverage for emergency outpatient and hospitalization care due to viral or bacterial illnesses and accidents (except stroke).
Hospital Access

🏥 Access care from 500+ accredited hospitals nationwide, excluding the following premium hospitals: Makati Med, St. Luke’s QC & BGC, Asian Hospital, The Medical City, Cardinal Santos, Manila Adventist, Notre Dame de Charles, Philippine Orthopedic Institute.

Coverage Includes
  • Room and board (Ward Room)
  • Doctors’ Fees
  • Lab and Diagnostics
  • Special treatments (up to ₱5,000 inner limits)
  • Medicines (except vaccines) as medically necessary during ER and confinement (except for cases declared as non-coverable, subject to standard inner limits)
  • Diagnostic and therapeutic procedures as medically necessary during ER and confinement
Additional Benefits
  • ✨ ₱50,000 Accidental Death & Disability Insurance
Conditions Covered
  • ✔️ Accidents and Injuries:
    • Injuries & fractures (except stroke)
    • Animal bites (first dose of vaccine only)
    • Burns, deep cuts needing suturing
    • Accidental chemical poisoning
  • ✔️ Acute Illnesses:
    • Appendicitis, Bronchitis, Gastritis, Gastroenteritis with dehydration
    • Pharyngitis, Pneumonia, Sinusitis, Tonsillopharyngitis with moderate dehydration
    • Upper Respiratory Tract Infection, Amoebiasis (initial episode), Anaphylactic Shock
    • Cellulitis, Chicken Pox with complications, Chikungunya, Cholera, Dengue Fever
    • Diphtheria, Leptospirosis, uncomplicated Malaria, Measles with high-grade fever
    • Meningitis, Pertussis, Polio, Systemic Viral Infections (with fever), Typhoid Fever
    • Urinary Tract Infection
Emergency Cases Include
  • Massive bleeding
  • Fractures or multiple injuries from accidents
  • Convulsions
  • Acute appendicitis
  • Severe dehydration (e.g., due to fever or diarrhea)
  • Fainting (Syncope)
Not Covered

Pre-existing conditions, injuries from misconduct, hazardous activities, military service, and other illnesses under PhilCare’s general exclusions.

DigiMed – What’s Included
  • ✅ One (1) medical tele-consultation through HeyPhil DigiMed with any of the following physicians:
    • 🩺 Family Medicine Specialists
    • 🩺 Internal Medicine
    • 🩺 General Practitioner
  • ✅ Issuance of medical assessment and prescription
  • ✅ Endorsement for lab tests/procedures to clinics if necessary
  • ✅ Referrals for specialty consult if necessary
  • ✅ Interpretation of laboratory test results
  • ✅ Consultations for pre-existing conditions not requiring emergency attention
Coverable Cases

This plan covers outpatient conditions that require primary care consultation but do not need physical examination or visualization by the doctor.

  • ✔️ Respiratory: allergies, asthma, bronchitis, colds/cough, sinusitis/rhinitis, pharyngitis, laryngitis, tonsillitis
  • ✔️ Cardiovascular follow-up: hypertension, medicine refills
  • ✔️ Gastrointestinal: diarrhea and vomiting, GERD, dyspepsia
  • ✔️ Skin conditions: allergies, rashes, insect bites, swelling/inflammation, cellulitis
  • ✔️ Other conditions: acne breakouts, arthritis, boils (pigsa), conjunctivitis, ear infections, fever, mild injuries, sprains and strains, UTI and bladder infections, general pediatric conditions for initial consult
Limitations

Not intended for cases requiring in-person examination or visualization.

  • Emergency-related cases
  • Maternity-related cases
  • Dental consultations
  • Mental health related conditions
  • Behavioral or developmental/psychiatric disorders and psychosomatic illness
  • Dermatology-related cases needing specialist care
  • Illnesses requiring treatment from a medical specialist (severe chronic conditions)
  • "Fit to work" assessments requiring medical certification
MindCare Counseling – What’s Included
  • ✔ One (1) Mind Care Counseling Session with MyGolana coaches and counselors
  • ✔ Fifty (50) minutes of personalized counseling time
  • ✔ Mental health screening and review of results
  • ✔ Counseling activities based on your needs, as advised by counselors
Coverable Cases
  • ✔️ Mental & Emotional Health: anxiety, depression, stress, loneliness, lack of motivation, frustration, fear, phobias, grief, low self-esteem, insomnia, anger management issues.
  • ✔️ Relationship & Family Issues: family and marital conflict, relationship fatigue, blended family challenges, bullying, domestic violence, gender issues, relational problems.
  • ✔️ Social & Environmental: living alone, social rejection, acculturation difficulties, phase-of-life transitions, neighbor conflict, housing/landlord issues.
  • ✔️ Educational & Work: academic or occupational problems.
  • ✔️ Economic & Basic Needs: financial struggles, poverty, lack of food or clean water.
  • ✔️ Health-Related: dealing with HIV.
Limitations of MindCare Counseling
  • Only covers access to HeyPhil Mind Care Coaches and Counselors.
  • Each session is 50 minutes and conducted entirely online.
  • Psychiatric consultations requiring in-person check-ups are not included.
  • No online diagnosis and prescription of psychiatric medication.
  • Not applicable to individuals:
    • Currently under psychiatric care or previously advised to seek psychiatric treatment
    • Mandated by authorities to undergo psychotherapy
    • In emergency or crisis (repeated suicidal ideation, risk of harm to self/others, known violent psychopathology, active or imminent suicide or harm attempt)
