Plan Overview

MediCard Select is an Administrative Services Only (ASO) program designed for individuals, families, and companies. Instead of paying a fixed HMO premium per member, you:

  • Set up a revolving healthcare fund with MediCard.
  • Use the fund to pay for eligible medical services availed within MediCard’s network.
  • May have unused fund refunded according to MediCard’s terms and conditions.

Note: Exact coverage, inclusions, and rules for fund usage and refund are governed by MediCard’s official MediCard Select documentation and agreement.

Key Features & Benefits
  • Revolving healthcare fund – starts at ₱30,000.00 per year, configured based on your needs (individual, family, or company).
  • Pay-as-utilized – the fund is used only when members avail eligible medical services.
  • Potential refund of unused fund at the end of the agreement period, as stated in MediCard’s terms.
  • Access to MediCard’s accredited providers and clinics for covered services, subject to the final agreement.
  • Can be structured for individuals, families, or groups of employees.

The exact list of covered services (e.g., consultations, ER, diagnostics, hospitalization, etc.) is defined in the signed MediCard Select ASO agreement and may vary per client.

General Notes & Exclusions

MediCard Select is a fund-based health program. While specific exclusions are defined in MediCard’s official policy documents, typical limitations may include:

  • Non-medically necessary services, purely cosmetic procedures, and experimental treatments.
  • Items or services not covered in the signed MediCard Select ASO agreement.
  • Benefits beyond the available revolving fund amount.
  • Other standard MediCard exclusions and limitations as stated in the MediCard Select terms and conditions.

For a complete and authoritative list of inclusions and exclusions, always refer to MediCard’s official MediCard Select brochure, contract, and policy wording.
