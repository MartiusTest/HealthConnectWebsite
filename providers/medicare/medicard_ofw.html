MediCard Kabayan – OFW Beneficiaries
Annual healthcare program for loved ones of overseas Filipinos
₱16,524.00 / year
Plan Overview

MediCard Kabayan is an annual healthcare program specifically designed for beneficiaries in the Philippines whose sponsor is working or living abroad. It aims to provide convenient, cashless healthcare access through MediCard’s accredited network.

Key Benefits
  • Annual healthcare coverage for the enrolled beneficiary, with structured limits based on the chosen Kabayan option (room type and benefit limits defined in the agreement).
  • In-patient hospital care in MediCard-accredited hospitals, including room and board, operating room, professional fees and medically necessary services, subject to plan limits.
  • Out-patient consultations with MediCard-accredited physicians, including referral to specialists and ordering of laboratory or diagnostic procedures when needed.
  • Emergency care services in accredited facilities, with validation that the case meets MediCard’s emergency criteria and is covered under the plan.
  • Access to MediCard’s nationwide network of accredited hospitals and clinics; specific coverage and limits per plan are detailed in the official Kabayan benefit schedule.
Who Pays?

The sponsor (usually the OFW) pays the annual membership fee (starting at ₱16,524.00 per year), and the beneficiary in the Philippines avails of covered services cashless at MediCard-accredited providers, subject to plan rules and limits.

Eligibility & Enrollment
  • Designed primarily for beneficiaries of overseas Filipinos (not just immediate family members, but also extended relatives or other designated individuals).
  • Coverage is annual and renewable, with membership and benefit limits defined in the Kabayan agreement.
  • Pre-existing conditions may be covered but are subject to specific limits and conditions in the benefit schedule.
How the Plan Works
  • Sponsor chooses a Kabayan option and lists the beneficiary/beneficiaries, then pays the membership fee.
  • For out-patient care, members can visit accredited hospitals/clinics and consult a primary accredited physician, who may request labs or refer to specialists.
  • For in-patient care, confinement must be recommended by a MediCard-accredited physician, and a Letter of Authorization (LOA) is issued prior to confinement whenever applicable.
  • All benefits are subject to the plan’s annual benefit limit and room type specified under the member’s Kabayan option.
Important Notes
  • Non-emergency elective surgeries and admissions may require prior review and approval by MediCard’s medical review board.
  • Coverage in non-accredited hospitals is generally not allowed, except under specific emergency reimbursement rules outlined in the agreement.
General Exclusions (Summary)

The complete exclusions are detailed in the official MediCard Kabayan Healthcare Program Agreement. The list below is a simplified summary for quick reference.

  • Hospitalization or treatment outside the Philippines (unless specifically covered for foreign emergencies in the schedule).
  • Services in non-accredited hospitals/clinics, except where the contract explicitly allows emergency reimbursements.
  • Non-medically necessary items such as private nurses, extra food/bed, TV, fans and other amenities unrelated to medical management.
  • Certain dermatologic, neurological or chronic conditions and other illnesses explicitly listed as excluded or limited in the benefit coverage.
  • Any case involving fraud, misrepresentation, or non-disclosure of material information may result in denial of benefits and/or termination of coverage.

For final and binding definitions, limits, and exclusions, always refer to the official MediCard Kabayan Agreement and its attached Benefit Coverage Schedule.
