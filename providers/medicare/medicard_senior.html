VIP Plan (Senior)
Comprehensive HMO-style coverage • Private room accommodation
₱25,379.00
Description

The VIP plan is a full-featured HMO program designed for members who prefer private-room confinement and broad access to MediCard’s accredited hospitals, clinics, and specialists.

Main Benefits
  • Inpatient coverage for medically necessary confinement in accredited hospitals
  • Private room and board (subject to policy limits)
  • Attending and specialist doctors’ fees (subject to schedule of benefits)
  • Required lab tests, diagnostics, and imaging during covered confinement
  • Outpatient consultations with accredited primary care and specialist doctors
  • Access to selected preventive and wellness services as defined by MediCard
Coverage Conditions
  • Admission must be medically necessary and recommended by an accredited physician
  • Confinement and procedures must take place in MediCard-accredited facilities
  • Some diagnostic tests and procedures may require prior approval
  • Benefit limits, sub-limits, and maximums apply based on the official VIP schedule of benefits
General Exclusions (Summary)

Refer to the official VIP brochure and policy for the complete list of exclusions. The plan typically excludes, among others:

  • Non-medically necessary or purely cosmetic procedures
  • Conditions related to self-inflicted injury or substance abuse
  • Investigational or experimental treatments not recognized as standard of care
  • Benefits beyond the agreed room type, benefit limits, or coverage region
Health Plus (Senior)
Clinic-based outpatient & preventive care
₱1,545.00
Description

Health Plus is focused on outpatient and preventive services delivered through MediCard free-standing clinics and partner facilities.

Main Benefits
  • Primary care consultations at MediCard clinics (as per plan limits)
  • Selected basic laboratory and diagnostic tests ordered by clinic doctors
  • Use of MediCard free-standing clinics for preventive check-ups and follow-ups
  • Access to health counseling and lifestyle advice where available
Coverage Conditions
  • Services must be availed at MediCard free-standing clinics or designated partners
  • Only medically necessary procedures and tests prescribed by clinic doctors are covered
  • Coverage is generally outpatient-only and does not include hospital confinement
Typical Exclusions
  • Hospital confinement and inpatient procedures
  • Major surgeries and high-cost diagnostics beyond the plan scope
  • Cosmetic procedures and non-medical services
  • Any service not included in the Health Plus program description or schedule of benefits
RxER (Senior)
Single-use emergency room coverage plus clinic benefits
₱2,378.00
Description

RxER is an emergency-focused program that provides a one-time emergency room benefit for specific cases, plus access to selected preventive and outpatient services at MediCard clinics.

Main Benefits
  • Emergency room coverage for defined emergency conditions (e.g., certain trauma cases)
  • Coverage for ER doctors’ fees, basic labs, diagnostics, and medicines within plan limits
  • Use of MediCard free-standing clinics for preventive care and minor outpatient services (as per official RxER brochure)
Coverage Conditions
  • Emergency event must meet MediCard’s definition of a covered emergency
  • Emergency treatment must occur at accredited hospitals and within the allowed time window
  • Benefit is generally single-use and subject to a fixed maximum limit
Typical Exclusions
  • Non-emergency consultations and elective procedures in the ER
  • Illnesses or injuries falling under standard MediCard general exclusions
  • Services beyond the specified benefit coverage or maximum amount
My MediCard (Senior)
Prepaid-style HMO access for seniors
₱3,600.00
Description

My MediCard is typically a prepaid card-type program that lets members access selected inpatient and outpatient benefits through MediCard’s accredited network, within predefined limits.

Main Benefits
  • Access to MediCard-accredited doctors and clinics for covered conditions
  • Coverage for medically necessary inpatient and outpatient services (subject to plan limits)
  • Use of card-like arrangement for convenient utilization of benefits
Coverage Conditions
  • Services must be availed at accredited MediCard facilities
  • Coverage is limited to the benefits and maximums defined by the My MediCard program
  • Some procedures or hospitalizations may require prior approval
Typical Exclusions
  • Non-medically necessary procedures and cosmetic treatments
  • Services beyond the specified benefit limits or card value
  • Other items listed under MediCard’s general exclusions
MediCard Select (Senior)
Premium coverage with enhanced limits
₱30,000.00
Description

MediCard Select is positioned as a more premium program with higher limits and access to a broader or more specialized hospital network compared to basic plans.

Main Benefits
  • Enhanced inpatient coverage with higher maximum limits
  • Access to selected hospitals and specialists under the Select network
  • Outpatient consultations and diagnostics within the Select benefit schedule
  • May include additional wellness or value-added services depending on MediCard’s design
Coverage Conditions
  • Use of accredited Select network hospitals and providers is typically required
  • Pre-approval may be necessary for major procedures and high-cost confinement
  • Benefit limits, inner limits, and co-payments (if any) follow the official Select schedule
Typical Exclusions
  • Services not included in the Select benefit schedule
  • Non-medical or elective procedures
  • Conditions falling under MediCard’s standard general exclusions
