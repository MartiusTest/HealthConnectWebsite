Open Roles

We are hiring for clinic, diagnostics, and insurance services.

Full-time • Part-time
Doctors

Provide consultations, treatment planning, and patient education.

  • Valid PRC license
  • Clinic or hospital experience
  • Patient-centered care
Medical Laboratory Technologists

Run diagnostic tests with accuracy and quality assurance.

  • PRC-registered MedTech
  • Lab instrumentation experience
  • Detail-oriented documentation
Radiologic Technologists

Support imaging services and patient preparation.

  • PRC-licensed RadTech
  • X-ray/ultrasound familiarity
  • Safety and patient comfort focus
Insurance Agents

Guide clients on coverage, benefits, and enrollment.

  • Strong communication skills
  • Sales or customer support background
  • Knowledge of health plans is a plus
Clinic & Support Staff

Keep the clinic running smoothly from front desk to operations.

  • Reception and scheduling support
  • Records and admin coordination
  • Reliable, service-oriented mindset
Private Nurses

Deliver bedside and home-based nursing care for patients.

  • Valid PRC nursing license
  • Home care or clinical experience
  • Compassionate patient support
Other Healthcare Specialists

  • Facility and logistics roles
